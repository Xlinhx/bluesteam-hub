# Research Note: Whole-Class Interactive Simulation Dynamics

## Insights from STEM Inquiry Education (PhET & Interactive Science)
1. **Interactive Sim vs. Passive Movie**: Simply projecting an animation on a screen results in passive consumption. Engagement skyrockets when the simulation incorporates **Predict -> Observe -> Discuss** pedagogical cycles.
2. **Teacher Command & Control**:
   - The teacher must have tools to pause the simulation, ask a quick question or prediction, hide or reveal labels, and highlight specific physical specimens.
   - Private teacher controls (answer keys, prompts, timing, roster) must remain isolated on the personal device and never be cast onto the communal screen.
3. **Public Projection Legibility**:
   - Standard desktop typography (12px–14px) is completely unreadable from the back of a 30-student classroom.
   - Presentation surfaces must utilize high-contrast, large readouts (24px–48px) and prominent physical dials that can be discerned from across a physical room.
