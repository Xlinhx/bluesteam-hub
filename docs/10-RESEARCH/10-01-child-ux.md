# Research Note: Pre-Reader UX & Direct Manipulation

## Key Findings from Educational Interaction Design
1. **Target Dimensions**: Research and playtesting with young children (ages 4–8) reveal that fine motor control is still developing. Small buttons (below 32px) cause mis-taps and frustration. Touch targets must remain at least **48×48px to 64×64px** for all core operations.
2. **Elimination of Text Prerequisite**: Text labels may exist as visual reinforcement for early readers, but primary comprehension must rely on spatial relationships, natural color gradients, iconic specimens, and directional motion.
3. **Drag vs. Single-Pointer Alternative (WCAG 2.2)**: Many young learners on trackpads or low-end touchscreens struggle with sustained drag operations. Every drag interaction must provide an equivalent single-tap source -> single-tap target selection flow.
4. **Immediate Causal Feedback**: When children manipulate a variable (e.g. dragging a depth slider), visual feedback must respond instantly (sub-50ms) to firmly anchor the causal relationship between action and environmental reaction.
